## Theme / Purpose

**Theme**  
Digital shadow theatre as cognitive mirror. The user confronts, multiplies, and plays with their own projected self through live camera + audio. Face slightly altered (morphic shadow), voice multiplied into choruses of possible selves. Slavic-guru edge: raw, irreverent, mythic self-confrontation without corporate wellness soft-focus.

**Purpose**  
Create an interactive browser experience that externalizes the shadow (Jungian + Slavic folk + digital) in real time. Camera-on face becomes a living Rorschach; captured voice becomes a swarm of echoes. The site is not a filter app — it is a temporary autonomous zone for projection, recognition, and playful dissolution of the single self.

**Core Loop**  
1. User grants camera + mic  
2. Live feed is continuously transformed (subtle face warp + shadow overlay)  
3. Voice is captured, fragmented, multiplied, re-timbred, and re-injected  
4. Shadows can be frozen, layered, shared, or merged with other users’ shadows  
5. Exit leaves no persistent identity trail (ephemeral by design)
