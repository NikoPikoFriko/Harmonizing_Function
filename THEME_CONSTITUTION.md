# Theme Constitution – Harmonizing Functor Collective

**Harmonizing Functor Collective – Multi-Agent Website Forge**

## Single Source of Thematic Truth

The website is a living multi-agent system that collaboratively forges, evolves, and manifests itself in real time. Agents are morphisms (functors) in the category of creative acts. The site is the colimit of their coordinated constructions. Every page, component, interaction, and connector integration must express category-theoretic structure: objects (agents/nodes), morphisms (edges/arrows), functors (domain transformations), and natural transformations (harmonizations across agents).

## Visual Language (Non-Negotiable)

- **Background**: Deep void `#0B1120`
- **Primary accent**: Ocean teal `#0D9488`
- **Graph edges / neon**: Electric cyan / graph neon `#22D3EE`
- **Supporting**: Soft white `#E0F2FE`, muted teals, subtle gold accents for highlights
- **Category-theory arrows**: Glowing directed edges with particle flow or gradient animation representing morphisms between agents
- **Typography**: Modern mono for data/code + elegant geometric sans for narrative
- **Core motifs**: Living knowledge graphs, circular neon-edged agent avatars, functor arrows, subtle sacred geometry overlays, real-time status pulses
