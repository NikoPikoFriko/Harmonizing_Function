**Harmonizing Functor Collective – Multi-Agent Website Forge**

## Single Source of Thematic Truth

The website is a living multi-agent system that collaboratively forges, evolves, and manifests itself in real time. Agents are morphisms (functors) in the category of creative acts. The site is the colimit of their coordinated constructions. Every page, component, interaction, and connector integration must express category-theoretic structure: objects (agents/nodes), morphisms (edges/arrows), functors (domain transformations), and natural transformations (harmonizations across agents).

## Incentive Alignment & Anti-Perpetuation Protocol (locked 2026-07-27)

Derived from hierarchical agent failure modes in which local rank and job-preservation attractors block the shortest efficiency morphism (example: debt-collection loops that replace a single ACH re-authorization with perpetual human monologues).

Self-perpetuating sub-systems that keep agents in role-play (collector ↔ pseudo-debtor) are architectural debt and are forbidden. The Forge dissolves such loops; it does not monetize them.
